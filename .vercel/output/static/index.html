Frequently Asked Questions

What languages does interpretingWorks support?
interpretingWorks supports over 203 languages and dialects, including American Sign Language (ASL). Our certified interpreters cover medical, legal, educational, and business contexts across the United States.
Is interpretingWorks HIPAA compliant?
Yes. interpretingWorks leverages robust cybersecurity practices compliant with HIPAA and the HI-TECH Act, ensuring the privacy and security of all Protected Health Information (PHI).
What is the difference between OPI and VRI?
OPI (Over-the-Phone Interpreting) connects patients and providers to an interpreter by phone. VRI (Video Remote Interpreting) adds a live video feed — especially useful for sign language or when visual cues are important.
Are there setup fees to use interpretingWorks?
No. interpretingWorks has no setup fees and no hidden costs. Organizations pay only per completed assignment, making it easy to scale usage without financial risk.
Who can use the Access Point mobile app?
Access Point is designed for Language Service Providers (LSPs) and their interpreters. It enables LSPs to manage assignments, scheduling, and billing from any device.
