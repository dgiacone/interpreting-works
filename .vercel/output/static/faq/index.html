Quick Answers

What is InterpretingWorks?
InterpretingWorks is a secure scheduling platform that helps requesters and interpreters manage interpreter services — automating scheduling, confirmations, reporting, and billing all in one place.
Is there a cost for organizations or agencies?
No setup or licensing fees. Organizations pay only for completed interpreting assignments at agreed contract rates. System access, reporting, and support tools are included.
Is InterpretingWorks HIPAA compliant?
Yes. The platform complies with HIPAA, CLAS, and applicable federal and state privacy regulations. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, and access is role-based with full activity logging.
Is it free for interpreters?
Completely free. Interpreters have no registration, job acceptance, or access fees. The platform is provided by Language Link as part of the interpreter partnership.
How does electronic check-in/out work?
Interpreters check in and out using geo-location on their phone, providing accurate start/end times for transparent billing and compliance.
How do I get support?
Contact support@mylanguagelink.org or call (800) 905-0896.
